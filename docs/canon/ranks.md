# The Ladder of Flesh

Prose lore only. Every number — standing thresholds, Council seat count, the officer
rank-index gate — lives in `addon/ChurchOfBeef/Data/Ranks.lua`, the single machine
canon. If a figure appears here, it is drift; delete it and read the data file.

The ladder is an addon-managed social layer. Standing makes a soul *eligible* for
elevation; a real guild officer speaks the word that makes it so.

## 1. Fresh Beef

Raw, unseasoned, and entirely welcome. Every soul enters the Church here — first-day
alt or decade-old veteran of other faiths — and the flesh must prove itself anew. There
is no special gate; Fresh Beef is the default intro, not a prize.

## 2. Cooked Beef

The first heat has been applied and the flock has noticed. Cooked Beef is early loyalty:
showing up with the congregation, building standing, and becoming someone the Church
recognizes. Running the addon is the digital on-ramp into that recognition; Discord is
community follow-up for the flock — never a lock on this rank.

## 3. Beef Knight

Sworn to the plate and reliably present. Standing makes a soul eligible for the Knighthood;
the Knighting of the Beef — the ceremony the Church expects — is the rite before elevation.
Knights are the members other members plan their evening around.

## 4. Beef Lord

A pillar of the congregation. Standing makes a soul eligible for the Lordship; the rite the
Church expects is victory in Beef Gora — a one-on-one contest against a sitting Beef Lord.
Lords set the tone in a group without being asked.

## 5. Beef Butcher

The highest grindable peak: a pure standing capstone. Raid loyalty feeds this rank, and no
rite stands between the faithful and the butcher's block. A Butcher has fed the Church so
long that their name is a fixture of the ladder — and here climbing by standing stops.

## 6. Beef Council

Governance, not grind. The Council is a seat-limited body appointed by the Beef Prophet —
policy, not points. Standing does not buy a chair; the Prophet places whom the Church needs.

## 7. Beef Prophet

Bestowed, never earned. The Prophet is the unique GM seat of the faith — anointed by
decree, not by standing. It is an event the guild remembers, not a rung the grind can reach.

## How elevation actually happens

Church titles on the ladder are roster-backed guild titles. The addon watches standing and
*suggests*; it never promotes. Officers see eligibility checklists in the queue — for Knight
and Lord, standing plus rite coaching (the Knighting of the Beef, Beef Gora) as expected
paths, not hard locks the machine enforces. Quest fellowship counts as loyalty alongside
instances. Officers dismiss a suggestion or correct standing in the addon; they elevate,
demote, seat the Council, and recognize the Prophecy in Blizzard's guild UI. Standings are
advisory testimony, so officer judgment — not the arithmetic — is the real gate.
