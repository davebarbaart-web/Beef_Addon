# The Ladder of Flesh

Prose lore only. Every number — standing thresholds, Council seat count, the officer
rank-index gate — lives in `addon/ChurchOfBeef/Data/Ranks.lua`, the single machine
canon. If a figure appears here, it is drift; delete it and read the data file.

The ladder is an addon-managed social layer. Standing makes a soul *eligible* for
elevation; a real guild officer speaks the word that makes it so.

## 1. Fresh Beef

Raw, unseasoned, and entirely welcome. Every soul enters the Church here, whether they
are a first-day alt or a decade-old veteran of other faiths — the flesh must prove
itself anew.

## 2. Cooked Beef

The first heat has been applied and the flock has noticed. This is the rank of a member
who has run with other Beef and come back for more, and it should arrive early enough
to feel like an invitation rather than a reward.

## 3. Beef Knight

Sworn to the plate and reliably present. Knights are the members other members plan
their evening around.

## 4. Beef Lord

A pillar of the congregation whose standing was earned over many nights, not one lucky
week. Lords are trusted to set the tone in a group without being asked.

## 5. Beef Butcher

The highest rank the grind alone can reach. A Butcher has fed the Church so long and so
faithfully that their name is a fixture of the ladder — and here the climbing by
standing stops.

## 6. Beef Council

Governance, not grind. The Council is a seat-limited body appointed directly by
officers, carrying no standing threshold at all; seats are scarce by design and their
number is configured in the data file, never here.

## 7. Beef Prophet

Bestowed, never earned. The Prophet is anointed by officer decree alone and is unique
by default — an event the guild remembers, not an inevitability the calendar produces.

## How elevation actually happens

The addon watches standing and *suggests*; it never promotes. Officers approve, reject,
demote, appoint to the Council, and bestow the Prophecy. Standings are self-reported
advisory testimony, so officer judgment — not the arithmetic — is the real gate.
