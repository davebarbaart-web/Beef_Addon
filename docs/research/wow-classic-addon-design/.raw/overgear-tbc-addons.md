### BigWigs

[BigWigs](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/big-wigs) is a lightweight boss mod addon that provides you with timers, alerts and warnings for important mechanics in dungeons and raids. It is a popular alternative to DBM and works especially well for players who would like to have a cleaner and less intrusive setup.

### Deadly Boss Mods

[Deadly Boss Mods](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/deadly-boss-mods) is one of the most popular PvE addons, providing you with timers, alerts and warnings for boss mechanics in virtually every dungeon and raid encounter pre-made and ready to use. It is easy to use right out of the box and you can also customize it even further if you want more control over how your notifications look.

### Details! Damage Meter

[Details! Damage Meter](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/details) is a powerful combat analysis addon which tracks damage, healing, threat, interrupts, deaths and much more. It is especially helpful if you want to get a better understanding of your performance and identify mistakes after a dungeon or raid pull.

### ClassicAuraDurations

[ClassicAuraDurations](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/classicauradurations) adds visible duration timers to debuffs and other effects, which makes them much easier to track during combat. Since the default TBC Anniversary UI doesn’t provide this information clearly, this addon is of great help in uptime and debuff management.

### ClassicSpellActivations

[ClassicSpellActivations](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/classicspellactivations) brings to the fore those abilities that are available through procs or special combat conditions. This made your rotation feel more fluid and helps you react more quickly when an important spell lights up.

### OmniCC

[OmniCC](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/omni-cc) adds readable numeric cooldowns timers to your abilities, items and action bars. This makes it much easier to see exactly when a spell or trinket will be ready again without having to guess.

### Clique

[Clique](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/clique) allows you to bind spells directly to mouse clicks which makes mouseover and click-casting much faster and more comfortable.It is particularly helpful to healers, but any player who wishes to have faster utility casts can benefit from it.

### Gargul

[Gargul](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/gargul) is a loot distribution addon which is frequently used in raids, particularly in pugs and organized group runs.It helps raid leaders manage rolls, assignments and loot decisions in a much cleaner and more transparent manner.

### Plater Nameplates

[Plater Nameplates](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/plater-nameplates) provides you with highly customizable enemy nameplates with improved visibility of casts, debuffs and other combat information. It is particularly helpful in dungeons and raids where target recognition can make a significant difference in a short period of time.

### Cell

[Cell](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/cell) is a modern replacement for the default party and raid frames and is much cleaner looking and allows for much more customization. It is particularly popular among healers as it makes it much easier to track health, debuffs and dispels.

### Pawn

[Pawn](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/pawn) is a simple gear comparison addon which helps you to quickly see if an item is an upgrade for your character. It is helpful for dungeon/raid preparation especially if you want to have quicker loot decisions without manually checking each of their stats.

### CharacterStatsClassic

[CharacterStatsClassic](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/characterstatsclassic) extends your character sheet by adding more detailed stat information such as stuff like hit & crit and mana or health reg. This will make it easier to understand your build and check to see if your stats are where they need to be.

### ItemRack Classic

[ItemRack Classic](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/itemrack-classic) lets you create and manage gear sets for various situations such as tanking, healing, DPS or farming. It also allows swapping of gears to be much quicker which is very useful in TBC Anniversary where many players carry multiple sets.

### Nova Instance Tracker

[Nova Instance Tracker](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/nova-instance-tracker) helps you to keep track of your dungeon lockouts, raid resets, and your recent instance activity. It is particularly useful if you farm dungeons frequently and wish not to lose track of your entries.

### ThreatClassic

[ThreatClassic](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/threatclassic2) is an addon for tracking threats and helps you keep an eye on your aggro levels in dungeons and raids. It is particularly important for tanks and DPS users as it helps avoid unnecessary pulls and maintain boss fights.

### VuhDo

[VuhDo](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/vuhdo) is a highly customizable raid frames addon and is particularly important for healer and support character users. It provides clean group frames, mouse-over functionality, and tracking for buffs and debuffs, making it easier for users to cast spells in dungeons and raids.

### TBC Anniversary Attunement Add-on

[![TBC Anniversary Top Add-Ons](https://overgear.com/guides/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/tbc-anniversary-attunement-addons.png)](http://overgear.com/guides/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/tbc-anniversary-attunement-addons.png)

For TBC Anniversary, attunements are the quest chains or requirements that, once completed, let you enter certain raids or unlock key progression content, but tracking them manually can be tedious. That’s where the [Attune](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/attune) addon comes in: it gives you a clear visual of your attunement progress, showing each step of the required quest lines and letting you see who else in your group or guild is on the same step.

To use the addon after installation (either manually or via an addon manager), just type /attune in your chat box to open the interface. From here, you’ll see a tree or list of all attunement tasks, with completed steps clearly marked and pending steps highlighted—this helps you plan what to do next.

You can also use the Survey feature to collect attunement status from your guildmates, which is especially useful when organizing raids or checking who’s ready for specific content. It even includes some extra tools like a raid planner that uses collected data to help organize groups based on attunement status, and you can customize character roles or statuses to improve coordination.

For best results, make sure guild members also have the addon installed so that survey and progress-sharing features work properly.

## Difference Between Add-ons and Weakauras

An addon in TBC Anniversary is a full-featured UI modification that adds new systems or replaces parts of the game interface, such as damage meters, boss mods, action bars, or inventory management. Addons are installed as separate folders, run continuously, and usually provide their own settings menus and long-term functionality.

A WeakAura, on the other hand, is a lightweight, highly customizable visual or audio trigger created inside the WeakAuras addon. WeakAuras are used to track very specific things like a cooldown, buff, debuff, proc, or resource, and show alerts only when certain conditions are met. In short, addons change how the game works overall, while WeakAuras give you precise, situation-based alerts on top of that.

To start using Weakauras, simply install the main addon, and then copy the import string from the aura you found interesting into the import section of the addon.
