# WoW Classic Patch 1.13.3 LUA API Change - Disabling Addons from Communicating in Custom Channels

[Classic](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/news?type=8)
Posted 2019/12/09 at 3:26 PM by [perculia](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/author/perculia)

With the Classic patch tomorrow, Blizzard is disabling the ability for addons to communicate in custom player-created channels. Addons can still communicate through Party, Raid, Guild, and Whisper, and Blizzard is looking into enabling support through Say and Yell as well.

Blizzard

[View Original](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/classic-patch-1-13-3-lua-api-change/384543)

With the release of World of Warcraft Classic 1.13.3 15, we’re making a change to disallow calling C\_ChatInfo.SendAddonMessage with the CHANNEL ChatType. This will prevent addons from communicating over custom, player-created chat channels.

Addons will still be able to communicate using PARTY, RAID, GUILD, and WHISPER in the same ways that they can now. Additionally, we are going to test a change to permit C\_ChatInfo.SendAddonMessage to be called with SAY and YELL, allowing your addons to communicate with players in your immediate vicinity.

We support using addons to customize your gameplay experience. However, we also want to ensure that we preserve the social dynamics that make Classic what it is. Ultimately, we decided that the ability for addons to send invisible messages to large, arbitrary groups of players all across Azeroth was something that risked undermining those social dynamics.

Thank you!

Addon does all the magic in background.

- addon inspects every player you meet (you should mouseover the player or target him, in inspect range), stores his PvP data in your local database, send this info to other addon users
