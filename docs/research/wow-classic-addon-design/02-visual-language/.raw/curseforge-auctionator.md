## Description

[![Donate](https://img.shields.io/badge/Support-Donate-red?style=for-the-badge)](https://www.curseforge.com/linkout?remoteUrl=https%253a%252f%252flinktr.ee%252fplusmouse)[![Discord (Comment)](https://shields.io/badge/discord-comment-lightblue?logo=discord&style=for-the-badge)](https://discord.gg/JabzHmzjWF)![AI Not Used](https://img.shields.io/badge/AI-Not_Used-green?style=for-the-badge)

Auctionator is designed for frequent and casual auction house users:

- Auction prices in item tooltips (with an AH full scan function to update the prices)
- Easy to use interface
- Protection against posting too low
- Crafting views show the cost of reagents and profits, with a button to find the wanted reagents on the AH.
- Searches containing lots of filters with a search history and organised into shopping lists
- Undercut scan and one-click cancelling for your auctions

# Updating prices

Go to the "Auctionator" tab in the Auction House and click the "Full Scan" button. On retail you can make this happen automatically when the AH is opened by turning the autoscan option on in the settings.

# Collecting Transmogs/Pets/… (Retail and Mists)

[Collectionator](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/collectionator) adds a new tab to the auction house which makes finding and buying uncollected items much easier.

# Activity Tracking

To keep track of AH usage, crafting orders, questing, mails, loot (and more) use [Journalator](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/journalator) .

# Did you know?

Right click any results column header to see extra information you can show, like the seller name.
