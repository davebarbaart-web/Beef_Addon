## Description

![logo](http://i.imgur.com/WjbOXzt.png)

_Most powerful, reliable, handsome, damage meter for World of Warcraft._

[****Support Details! and get exclusive content: patreon.com/detailsaddon**** \\
**Details! does work on Retail WOW and implements new data not available on Blizzard Damage Meter.**](https://www.patreon.com/detailsaddon)

Details! updates in real time all the combat data. Do not trust sources saying it only update after combat.

Details! has a very small cpu usage compared to Blizzard damage meter solution, this can reach up to 6x faster and less framerate drops.

- Guild Ranking: store the damage and heal from guild runs, build lists of top DPS on each boss.
- Talents and Item Level Tracker: see which talents raid members are using and what is their item level.
