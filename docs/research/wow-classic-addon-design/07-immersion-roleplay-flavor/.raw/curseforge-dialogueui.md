## Description

#### **Features**

- Select options and accept quests by pressing the **keyboard** or **gamepad** button.
- Control **camera motion** to enhance immersion. (You can disable camera movement in the settings)
- **Adjustable font size** to increase readability.
- Copy dialogue texts and NPC chat messages.
- Display the lore text when you pick up a quest item.
