# 12 — Classic vs Retail UI Philosophy & Player Expectations

**Track date:** 2026-07-25  
**Scope:** Design philosophy only — no addon code.  
**Product lens:** [Church of Beef V1 design spec](../../../superpowers/specs/2026-07-25-church-of-beef-design.md) (TBC Classic Anniversary social/guild spectacle addon).

## Why this track exists

Church of Beef sits in a culture war Blizzard never fully resolved: Classic players want *feeling* preserved, not just patch numbers. UI and addons are where that war is fought daily. This folder maps what the audience rejects, what they quietly use anyway, and what that means for a new Classic-native brand.

## One-line thesis

Classic players reject addons that **automate decisions or erase social friction**; they accept addons that **reduce tedium without removing agency**. Aesthetic restraint beats Retail chrome — but information overlays (Questie, WA, DBM) are already “allowed culture.”
