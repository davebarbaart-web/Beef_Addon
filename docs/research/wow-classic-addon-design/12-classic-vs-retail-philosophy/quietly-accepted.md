## 1. The quiet rule

Despite #NoChanges rhetoric, Classic players broadly accept tools that:

- Reduce **tedium** (bags, AH listing, chat noise)
- Surface **information** the player still acts on (quest pins, boss timers, WA icons)
- Stay **opt-in** and uninstallable
- Do **not** press buttons, move the character, or replace social negotiation

Blizzard Watch’s Wrath addon primer treats DBM, Auctionator, Questie, TomTom, Details!, and WeakAuras as normal recommendations — while noting you *can* play without addons ([best addons for Wrath Classic](https://blizzardwatch.com/2022/10/24/best-addons-use-wrath-classic)). Community “must-have QoL” threads on r/classicwow continue that pattern ([example thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/classicwow/comments/1gunhsq/what_are_your_musthave_quality_of_life_addons_for/)).

## 3. WeakAuras as the gold-standard pattern

WeakAuras wins Classic culture because it matches the accepted ethic:

- **Declarative:** if condition → show icon/text  
- **Player-owned:** imports are choices, not forced HUD  
- **Low chrome:** output is usually small auras, not a floating Retail app shell  
- **Information, not automation**

**CoB borrow:** Ceremony/rank toasts should feel WA-like — short-lived, center-weighted, then gone — not a permanent dashboard widget in V1. Spec already points this direction (§10; visual language notes in library doc 03).

## 4. Questie: accepted at population scale

Questie is the strongest proof that Classic players will adopt Meaning-B QoL en masse while still performing purity theater in forums.

- Market position: “The Classic Quest Helper” with enormous download counts ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/questie)).
- Editorial framing: fills missing retail-style map guidance without changing combat/progression systems ([NoobToBoss](https://noobtoboss.com/addon/questie/); [questiewowclassic.com](https://questiewowclassic.com/why-questie-is-essential-wow-classic-guide/)).
- Cons listed even by advocates: map clutter; some prefer pure experience.

**CoB borrow:** Clarity of icons and readable tooltips > ornament. A ladder that is scannable like Questie’s map language will feel “Classic useful,” not “Retail shiny.”

## 5. What “quietly accept” means for social/guild addons

Social addons have less precedent than combat QoL, but Total RP 3’s positioning is instructive: powerful customization + **clean, accessible UI** — not Retail Edit Mode cosplay ([Total RP 3 on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/total-rp-3) — marketing language widely cited in RP communities for restraint + depth).

Quietly accepted social patterns:

- Profiles / notes / descriptions  
- Optional nameplate/tooltip hooks  
- Guild-centric windows opened on demand  
- Interoperability with other RP tools rather than hard lock-in  

Quietly rejected social patterns:

- Always-on overlay chrome  
- Automation of invites/roles  
- Paywalled “guide leash” culture (see HC RestedXP fights)

## 7. Acceptance test for new CoB ideas

Ask:

1. Would a Questie user recognize this as **info + agency**?  
2. Would a #NoChanges poster accuse this of **playing the game for them**?  
3. Can a guildmate without the addon still share the moment (chat line, ceremony in world)?  

If (1) yes, (2) no, (3) yes — ship. That is the quietly-accepted lane.
