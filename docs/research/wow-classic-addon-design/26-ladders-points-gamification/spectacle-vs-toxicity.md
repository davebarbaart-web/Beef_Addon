# Spectacle vs toxicity

**Track:** 26 · **Scope:** How public ranking creates fun rivalry — and when it becomes identity harm, favoritism drama, or grind shame.

## Spec’s spectacle bet

V1 explicitly measures success by **visible guild-chat reaction** to a promotion, not only by correct commits ([§13 social check](../../../superpowers/specs/2026-07-25-church-of-beef-design.md)). Demotion / “excommunication” is first-class comedy content. Gora losses pay so challenges stay alive.

That is the right north star: **theater > optimization**.

## Why leaderboards cut both ways

### Engagement engine

Leaderboards turn private scores into social competition and spawn parallel goals (beat neighbor / reach top) ([UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/building-better-leaderboards-a5013d19cbd7)). Workplace board data shows most surviving setups are a **single ranked metric**, not elaborate badge trees ([Leaderboarded: gamification leaderboards](https://leaderboarded.com/blog/posts/gamification-leaderboards)).

### Harm modes

Documented failure modes in gaming communities ([Leaderboarded: Gaming leaderboards and toxic behavior](https://leaderboarded.com/blog/posts/leaderboards-in-gaming/)):

| Failure | Mechanism |
|---------|-----------|
| Rank as identity | Title becomes insult / status caste |
| Endless grind | Season resets + time-as-skill punish casuals |
| Unfair axes | Hours played ≠ skill or “faith” |
| Cheating pressure | Boosts, edits, forged state |
| Bottom invisibility | Only top 0.1% celebrated → everyone else checks out |

Social comparison is a core psychological drive; leaderboards amplify it. Studies show mixed motivation effects; **leaderboards-only** treatments can feel more negative than boards paired with other recognition (e.g. badges) ([PMC8916940](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8916940/)). Experiments isolating competition components find **performance feedback** often drives upside more than raw comparison ([Belo et al. PDF](https://rodrigobelo.com/assets/pdf/leaderboards.pdf)).

Toxic comparison (clinical framing): comparison becomes harmful when it is excessive, identity-threatening, or chronic ([Onward: toxic comparison](https://www.onwardpsychservices.com/blog/what-is-toxic-comparison)).

### Classic guild loot politics (warning, not blueprint)

DKP/EPGP threads show players blaming systems for favoritism, RMT-tinged “bonus points,” and loot lockouts — while defenders say **leadership and transparency** decide toxicity, not the acronym ([Blizzard forum](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dkp-and-epgp-systems/1791081)). Beef inherits the same officer-power surface; comedy does not exempt it.

## Mitigations that fit Church of Beef

| Mitigation (from research / practice) | Beef mapping |
|---------------------------------------|--------------|
| Multiple ladders / casual + ranked | Social ladder only — never combat MMR |
| Team / guild shared goals | Instance co-play with guildmates; ceremonies |
| Celebrate more than the top | Toasts for suggestions, Gora participation, first Cooked Beef |
| Behavior affects standing | Officer demote; reject absurd suggestions |
| Performance feedback > pure rank shame | My Standing event log (“why you scored”) |
| Pair recognition tools | Titles + chat announce + ceremony — not points alone |
| Opt-in pressure | `/beef` + minimap; no forced overlay |
| Transparency | Officer sees breakdown; guild hears announces |

## Comedy framing rules (product voice)

1. **Titles are costumes, not MMR.** Copy that frames ranks as scripture/job titles, not “elo.”
2. **Losses are content.** Public Gora loss with participation credit is anti-toxicity design.
3. **Demotion is a bit, not a silent SV edit.** Announce it; own the spectacle.
4. **Never shame hours played.** Caps exist so no-lifing cannot buy Prophet.
5. **Prophet/Council scarcity** prevents “solved the addon” supremacy in month one.
6. **Reject without contempt.** Clearing a suggestion should feel bureaucratic-funny, not like a loot council veto of a friend’s worth.

## Red lines (ship blockers for tone)

- Sorting the Ladder tab primarily by raw standing (turns church into DKP sheet).
- Public “worst beef” / shame boards.
- Auto-shaming AFK or low attendance in guild chat.
- Pay-to-win or real-money standing.
- Letting non-officers broadcast forged commits (already a security rule — also a toxicity rule).

## Success metric (keep from spec)

If a promotion happens on a guild night and **nobody reacts in chat**, the ladder failed as spectacle even if sync is perfect. Design UI and announce text for that reaction, not for spreadsheet completeness.
