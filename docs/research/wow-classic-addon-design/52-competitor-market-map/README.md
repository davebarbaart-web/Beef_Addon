## Executive insights

1. **Raid loot ops are saturated.** Core Loot Manager (DKP/EPGP/SK), RCLootCouncil Classic (~9.1M downloads), Gargul (SoftRes/GDKP/TMB), and CEPGP already own fairness, bidding, and ML workflows. Beef must not look like another loot system.
2. **Attendance is a feature of other products, not a category.** It rides on WebDKP awards, Guilds of WoW Discord/calendar RSVPs, and calendar helpers — rarely a standalone “devotion” product.
3. **Web + Discord platforms sit above the addon layer.** [Guilds of WoW](https://guildsofwow.com/), [softres.it](https://softres.it/), [wowaudit](https://www.wowaudit.com/), Raider.IO, and Warcraft Logs Classic solve recruitment, RSVPs, audits, and soft-reserves out of game. Beef’s fully in-game V1 is a deliberate contrast, not a competitor to their web stacks.
4. **Guild “fun” is thin and under-branded.** Recognition tools (e.g. Guild Achieve Congratulator) and greeting helpers exist, but none ship a persistent theatrical rank institution with stream spectacle. That gap is Beef’s lane.
5. **RP directories own character identity, not guild standing.** TRP3/MRP (see track 13) are peer profile/title systems. Beef titles are guild-ladder status with Officer+ authority — adjacent fantasy, different product.
6. **Hybrid officer-gated ladders are rare outside loot.** Suggest → Officer approve for *social* standing (not EP/GP or DKP) is the clearest white space relative to CLM/RCLC/WebDKP.
