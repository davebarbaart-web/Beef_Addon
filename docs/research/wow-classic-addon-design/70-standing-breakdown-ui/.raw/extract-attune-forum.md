Hi all,

I’m working on an addon to help with the long attunement chains present everywhere in TBC.

The addon gives you a visual representation of where you are in the attunement chain, what the next steps are and what you’ll need.

It also allows you to ‘survey’ your guild (and groups/raids) to know where they are at and know who is on the same attunement step as you.

Sample Screenshot here: `https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/329/390/attune.PNG`

I have a working copy on curseforge, that works on WoW Classic, with all the Classic and TBC attunement chains in it:

Curseforge: `https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/attune`

Would love if people could give it a test and come back to me with all the bugs/comments/suggestions they have.

My Addon Development Discord: `https://discord.gg/MpfDeBZ`

Thanks

Not really. In order to do the Kara quest, you need to go to Shadow Labs. This instance is locked and in order to enter Shadow Labs you first must do Sethekk Halls to get the key. You also need to go to the Arcatraz, which is also a locked instance and requires a whole Netherstorm questchain to obtain. Only one person needs the key to unlock it, but someone has to have done these things (or you need a max skill lockpicking rogue).

Getting attuned to TK requires Shattered Halls - also a locked instance that requires killing the Fel Reaver. The lock could be picked, but the quest must be done on heroic difficulty, and in order to enter a heroic dungeon all group members must have the heroic key, which requires revered status with the applicable faction. Then heroic Steamvaults, heroic Shadow Labs, and heroic Arc. Again, each requires revered status with different factions. Then you need to kill Magtheridon.

For SSC Attunement, you need to heroic Slave Pens, Nightbane in Kara, and Gruul.

There are multiple stages to each attunement, including pre-requisites such as all group members having revered status with every faction and having obtained keys to locked instances.

I for one would love an easy way to track all of these things.
