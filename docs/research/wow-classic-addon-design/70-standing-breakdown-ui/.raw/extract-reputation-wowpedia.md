You can gain or lose favor, otherwise known as **reputation**, with many of the several different factions in Azeroth. Higher reputation gives access to special rewards or new quests to accomplish. You can increase reputations using several methods, including:

- Completing quests aligned with the faction, including [repeatable reputation quests](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Repeatable_Reputation_Quest "Repeatable Reputation Quest").
- Killing certain creatures.
- Doing level-appropriate dungeons while wearing a faction's [Championing](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Championing "Championing") tabard.
- Using writs purchased from the [Argent Tournament](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Argent_Tournament "Argent Tournament") (available at level 80) and [Molten Front](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Molten_Front "Molten Front") (available at level 85).

...or Horde

Reputation is very similar to experience. Reputation is divided into a number of different levels for which players must earn reputation points to progress through. Higher reputation levels generally require more points than the previous level to progress. The exception is [hated](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Hated "Hated"), which spans a very large number of reputation points. Reputation points, just like experience points, are gained through quests and killing various mobs. Unlike experience, it is possible to lose reputation points with some factions, either by killing members of the faction or by assisting rival factions. For rival factions, gaining reputation with one will normally decrease reputation with the other, but at a greater rate. For instance, if you kill a Booty Bay Bruiser, you will gain 25 points of reputation with the Bloodsail Buccaneers, but lose 125 reputation with Booty Bay. Currently there are 4 of these rival-faction pairs: [Aldor](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Aldor "Aldor") (Draenei) vs. [Scryers](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Scryers "Scryers") (Blood Elves), [Steamwheedle Cartel](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Steamwheedle_Cartel "Steamwheedle Cartel") (Goblins) vs. [Bloodsail Buccaneers](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Bloodsail_Buccaneers "Bloodsail Buccaneers") (pirates), [Ravenholdt](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Ravenholdt "Ravenholdt") (rogues) vs. [Syndicate](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Syndicate "Syndicate") (Thugs), and [The Oracles](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Oracles "The Oracles") (Gorlocs) vs. [Frenzyheart Tribe](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Frenzyheart_Tribe "Frenzyheart Tribe") (Wolvar).

Various quests and faction-specific rewards are available across the levels, but the player must be able to interact with the faction NPCs to gain access to these rewards. An NPC's faction is shown under their name. This usually requires [Neutral](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Neutral "Neutral") or better standing.

_NOTE: All NPCs with whom you have Friendly or higher reputation will appear [green](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Green "Green") in game. The level colors above are only used in Wowpedia and in the [Armory](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Armory "Armory")._

Any player who comes close to anyone of this [faction](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Faction "Faction") will be attacked on sight.

Players start off at Hated with the [Brood of Nozdormu](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_of_Nozdormu "Brood of Nozdormu"), [Netherwing](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Netherwing "Netherwing"), [Sons of Hodir](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Sons_of_Hodir "Sons of Hodir"), [Therazane](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Therazane_(faction) "Therazane (faction)") and [Syndicate](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Syndicate "Syndicate") factions, having 0/36000 reputation with these factions, the lowest reputation possible in the game.

While not shown in the character window, the output of [API UnitReaction](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/API_UnitReaction "API UnitReaction") suggests that [Horde](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Horde "Horde") players have a Hated reputation with [Alliance](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Alliance "Alliance") factions and vice-versa.

When you click on friendly [NPCs](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/NPC "NPC"), their name and highlight will be green. Certain factions in [Northrend](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Northrend "Northrend") sell [tabards](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Tabard "Tabard") for players who become friendly. ### Exalted\[ [Sign in to edit](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fwowpedia.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FReputation%3Faction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

The reputation level ![Rep exalted icon 18x18](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowpedia/images/1/17/Rep_exalted_icon_18x18.png/revision/latest?cb=20071223211233)Exalted is the highest reputation level possible, just above Revered.

With this reputation level and additional restrictions (see [Purchasing other mounts](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Mount#Purchasing_other_mounts "Mount")), you can purchase and train to ride mounts of another [race](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Race "Race"). It also gives a 20% discount on items. Certain factions sell [tabards](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Tabard "Tabard") for players who become exalted.

Gaining reputation with a Faction City you are already exalted with will cause 25% of the reputation to "bleed over" to your next-highest city reputation. This does not happen when you gain reputation with all cities at once (ie, "Horde +250")- in this case, the reputation that would go to already-exalted cities is simply lost.

### Paragon\[ [Sign in to edit](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fwowpedia.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FReputation%3Faction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

_Main article: [Paragon reputation](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Paragon_reputation "Paragon reputation")_

Paragon is an additional reputation level introduced in _[Legion](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion "World of Warcraft: Legion")_. After reaching [Exalted](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Exalted "Exalted") with an eligible faction, a bag icon will appear on the faction's reputation bar. Hovering over this icon displays an extra reputation bar that can be filled repeatedly.

Filling the Paragon bar will trigger an [emissary quest](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Emissary_system "Emissary system") which gives resources and rewards depending on the faction and expansion. Each cache has a chance to contain an exclusive [toy](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Toy "Toy"), [pet](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Pet "Pet") or even a [mount](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Mount "Mount").

Based on the _[Shadowlands](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Shadowlands "World of Warcraft: Shadowlands")_![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowpedia/images/4/40/Inv_misc_covenant_renown.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/16?cb=20201228171631)[\[Renown\]](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Renown_(Shadowlands)) system, Renown used for the _[Dragonflight](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Dragonflight "World of Warcraft: Dragonflight")_ factions replaces the general reputation system with rewards that come with every Renown level, with each point needing 2,500. ## Reputation sheet\[ [Sign in to edit](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fwowpedia.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FReputation%3Faction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

You can check your reputation with all factions by pressing the "u" hotkey or by going to the Character window ("c" hotkey) and clicking on the Reputation tab along the bottom. You can also enable or disable various options:

- **Show as experience bar:** Allows you to display one reputation bar as an experience bar on your main display (located above your level experience bar).
- **[At War](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/At_War "At War"):** If checked, you can initiate combat with NPCs of that faction, and your AoE spells will affect the mobs of that faction. It's always checked if you are Hated or Hostile; it can't be checked for other races within the Alliance/Horde.
- **Inactive:** Moves the selected faction to the bottom of the reputation pane.

## Gaining and losing reputation\[ [Sign in to edit](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fwowpedia.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FReputation%3Faction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Completing non-repeatable [quests](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Quests "Quests") will usually increase your reputation with the quest giver's faction(s) by a fixed number of points. Many of the factions also have special repeatable quests that give reputation when completed, or special mobs that give reputation when killed. If the faction has a [Championing](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Championing "Championing") tabard (which will be available from the faction's [Quartermaster](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Quartermaster "Quartermaster")), you can gain reputation with that faction by wearing the tabard in dungeons. These dungeons must be either at your level or at the level indicated on the tabard to give reputation gains. Often the [Championing](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Championing "Championing") tabard requires [Friendly](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Friendly "Friendly") reputation with that faction to purchase. If that is the case, you will be able to get to [Friendly](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Friendly "Friendly") with them by doing quests given by questgivers belonging to that faction (as indicated under the questgiver's name).

Reputation with certain factions can also be gained by turning in writs, which can be gotten as rewards from quests in the [Argent Tournament](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Argent_Tournament "Argent Tournament") or purchased with currency at the [Molten Front](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Molten_Front "Molten Front").

Grey mobs used to give 20% of the normal reputation until the release of Patch 3.0.8. Now any mob will give full reputation. See the [mob difficulty colors](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Mob_difficulty_colors "Mob difficulty colors") chart for when mobs transition from green to grey.

As noted above, killing certain mobs and doing certain quests will increase your reputation with one faction but decrease it with another. Currently there are 4 rival faction pairs, listed above.

For a few factions, there are limits on the methods to gain reputation. For instance, for [Cenarion Expedition](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Cenarion_Expedition "Cenarion Expedition"), killing certain mobs in Zangarmarsh only gives reputation until reaching [Friendly](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Friendly "Friendly"). At [Friendly](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Friendly "Friendly") or higher, the mobs give no reputation and alternative methods must be used to gain further reputation. For best results when trying to reach Exalted with a faction, see the page of the relevant reputation before completing quests for them.

## Gaining reputation with the Horde or Alliance factions\[ [Sign in to edit](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fwowpedia.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FReputation%3Faction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Every playable race has a home faction, for example Darnassus for night elves, Orgrimmar for orcs, etc.

Gaining reputation with your home faction provides access to discounted prices at their vendors, and certain reputation [achievements](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Achievement "Achievement"). However, gaining reputation with other factions may provide additional benefits, such as specific items, patterns, spells etc which can be purchased from vendors of that faction at certain reputation levels. In addition, if you want a mount of a faction that is not your own, you must be [Exalted](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Exalted "Exalted") with them. For example, if you are a troll and you want to buy a wolf mount, you must be [Exalted](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Exalted "Exalted") with Orgrimmar.

Most quests for an Alliance or Horde faction will give full reputation gain for that faction, and one quarter of that reputation gain for the other factions on that side. For example, a quest completed for a Stormwind NPC will give 250 Stormwind reputation and 62.5 reputation for the other Alliance factions. This "spillover" reputation gain is capped at "friendly" (5999/6000); [\[1\]](http://us.battle.net/wow/en/blog/1232869) that is, if you complete a quest for one Alliance or Horde faction, you will gain spillover reputation for every other faction with which you are friendly or below, but this spillover reputuation gain will not give you the last point to tip any of those reputations over to "exalted".

When you are friendly or better with any of your home factions, you can purchase a tabard from that faction's quartermaster. Wearing the tabard allows you to gain reputation by [championing](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Championing "Championing") in any dungeons which are near your level (the exception is Burning Crusade dungeons, where you will gain Burning Crusade reputations instead).

At level 77 and above, characters can also gain reputation with their faction's races by completing quests for the [Argent Tournament](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Argent_Tournament "Argent Tournament"). Each Valiant quest rewards 250 reputation points for the appropriate race, and 62.5 for each of the other races in the faction. (e.g. Completing a daily quest as a Valiant of the Undercity gives 250 Undercity reputation, 62.5 Orgrimmar, 62.5 Thunder Bluff, etc.) In addition, the Champion daily quests can optionally award a ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowpedia/images/9/9e/Inv_scroll_11.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/16?cb=20180824084702)[\[Champion's Writ\]](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Champion%27s_Writ), which can be exchanged for 250 reputation with any of the home cities. Similarly, at level 85, Writs of Commendation from the major city factions can be bought at the [Molten Front](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Molten_Front "Molten Front").

By default you are the equivalent of [Hated](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Hated "Hated") with the opposite team ( [Horde](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Horde "Horde") if you are [Alliance](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Alliance "Alliance"); [Alliance](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Alliance "Alliance") if you are [Horde](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Horde "Horde")). There is no way to increase or even view your reputation with factions of the opposite team (ex: You cannot increase your Undercity reputation while playing an Alliance character).

[![Wrath-Logo-Small](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowpedia/images/c/c1/Wrath-Logo-Small.png/revision/latest?cb=20090403101742)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Wrath_of_the_Lich_King "World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King")**This section concerns content related to _[Wrath of the Lich King](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Wrath_of_the_Lich_King "World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King")_.**

_Main article: [Wrath of the Lich King reputation rewards](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Wrath_of_the_Lich_King_reputation_rewards "Wrath of the Lich King reputation rewards")__See also: [championing](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Championing "Championing")_

For the most part, the reputations from Classic WoW and from Burning Crusade are not re-used in the Wrath of the Lich King. In the same way that [Cenarion Circle](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Cenarion_Circle "Cenarion Circle") of Azeroth turned into [Cenarion Expedition](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Cenarion_Expedition "Cenarion Expedition") in Outland, several old factions have new reputations. For example, the [Argent Dawn](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Argent_Dawn "Argent Dawn") made its way to Northrend, but since it has allied with the Knights of the Silver Hand, they are called The [Argent Crusade](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Argent_Crusade "Argent Crusade").

However, for [achievements](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Achievement "Achievement"), many old reputations received new attention. Nearly all factions have an achievement tied to them, and there are even additional achievements for pairs or groups of factions. For instance, acquiring [exalted](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Exalted "Exalted") with both [Cenarion Circle](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Cenarion_Circle "Cenarion Circle") and the [Cenarion Expedition](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Cenarion_Expedition "Cenarion Expedition") will get you the title of ![](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowpedia/images/4/43/Ability_racial_ultravision.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/16?cb=20180824003316)[\[Guardian of Cenarius\]](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Guardian_of_Cenarius). See [Achievements](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Achievement "Achievement") for more details.

- [![Bc icon](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowpedia/images/0/0e/Bc_icon.gif/revision/latest?cb=20110218184702)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_The_Burning_Crusade "World of Warcraft: The Burning Crusade")**[Patch 2.3.0](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Patch_2.3.0 "Patch 2.3.0") (2007-11-13):** Vendor Discounts: All vendors, flightmasters, and repairs with an associate faction now give discounts at all levels above neutral: (Friendly: 5%, Honored: 10%, Revered: 15%, Exalted: 20%).
_Previously there was a flat 10% discount starting at [honored](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Honored "Honored")._

- [![Bc icon](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowpedia/images/0/0e/Bc_icon.gif/revision/latest?cb=20110218184702)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_The_Burning_Crusade "World of Warcraft: The Burning Crusade")**[Patch 2.3.0](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Patch_2.3.0 "Patch 2.3.0") (2007-11-13):** The requirement for Heroic Keys has been lowered to [honored](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Honored "Honored").
_Previously required [revered](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Revered "Revered")._

- [![WoW Icon update](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowpedia/images/3/38/WoW_Icon_update.png/revision/latest?cb=20180602175550)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/World_of_Warcraft "World of Warcraft")**[Patch 1.7.0](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Patch_1.7.0 "Patch 1.7.0") (2005-09-13):** Reputation gains given to a player on the Alliance, Horde, or Steamwheedle Cartel factions directly will now cascade down to member factions through the middle of the Exalted reputation level.

- [![WoW Icon update](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowpedia/images/3/38/WoW_Icon_update.png/revision/latest?cb=20180602175550)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/World_of_Warcraft "World of Warcraft")**[Patch 1.3.0](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Patch_1.3.0 "Patch 1.3.0") (2005-03-07):** Players under Mind Control pass reputation changes due to kills to the controlling player, and do not receive the reputation changes themselves.
